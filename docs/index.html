Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Athlete Recovery Tracker is an independently developed project that helps users review athletic recovery, training, and wellness information. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by WHOOP.

Information accessed

If you authorize a WHOOP connection, the application may access recovery scores, heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, sleep metrics, physiological cycles, and workout data, according to the permissions you grant. You may also choose to enter training sessions, soreness, pain, wellness ratings, and notes.

How information is used

Information is used to display your metrics, visualize trends, and support recovery-tracking features. The application is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

Storage and security

When integration features are enabled, authorized information may be processed and stored by the application's backend and database. Service providers used for hosting and storage may process this information to operate the service. Access credentials and authorization tokens are intended to be kept private and protected using reasonable technical safeguards. No online service can guarantee complete security.

Sharing

The project does not intend to sell personal information or share it with advertisers. Data may be processed by technical service providers necessary to operate the application.

Your choices and deletion

You can revoke the application's WHOOP access using the authorization controls available through WHOOP. To request access to or deletion of any information stored by the application, contact the developer using the contact information below. Revoking WHOOP access does not necessarily delete previously stored information; deletion can be requested separately.

Policy changes

This policy may change as the project develops. Updates will be published here with a revised date.
